# Legal references used in the complaint templates

Every draft Kavach produces ends with "Verify section numbers with the portal or a lawyer before filing." This file records where each reference comes from and how confident we are. Checked 24 Oct 2026.

| Reference in the draft | What it is | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rule 3(2)(b), IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules 2021, as amended | 2-hour removal of complaints about content showing nudity or a sexual act, and electronic impersonation, including morphed images and deepfake sexual imagery of a person without consent | iPleaders, "IT Rules 2026" (https://blog.ipleaders.in/it-rules-2026/); MeitY notification G.S.R. 120(E) | Medium. The 2-hour figure and rule number appear in the iPleaders analysis and in the hackathon brief's Outlook Money citation; we did not read the gazette text directly. |
| Amendment dates: notified 10 Feb 2026, in force 20 Feb 2026 | | Medianama (https://www.medianama.com/2026/02/223-meity-amendments-it-rules-synthetic-media-deepfakes/), Mondaq, Hogan Lovells | High. All sources agree. |
| Rule 3(1)(d), 3-hour takedown on government or court order | Not cited in the drafts; mentioned on the About page as context | Medianama, Hogan Lovells | High. |
| IT Act 2000, Section 66E | Punishment for violation of privacy (capturing or publishing images of private areas without consent) | Statute | High. |
| IT Act 2000, Section 67 | Publishing or transmitting obscene material in electronic form | Statute | High. |
| IT Act 2000, Section 67A | Publishing or transmitting material containing sexually explicit act | Statute | High. Used only when moderation flags explicit content. |
| Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023, Section 77 | Voyeurism, including dissemination of a woman's image captured in a private act without consent (replaces IPC 354C) | advocatekhoj.com bare act, vidhijudicial.com | High. |
| BNS 2023, Section 351 | Criminal intimidation (chapter "Of criminal intimidation, insult and annoyance", 351 to 355) | Wikipedia chapter table; bare act | Medium-high. The chapter range is confirmed; 351 is the criminal intimidation section in the bare act. |
| BNS 2023, Section 356 | Defamation | Wikipedia chapter table ("Of Defamation (356)") | High. |

Not cited, considered: BNS Section 75 (sexual harassment) and Section 79 (insult to the modesty of a woman). They may apply but were left out of the default drafts to avoid overclaiming; a lawyer can add them.

Portal and helpline: National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, https://cybercrime.gov.in, category "Women/Child related crime". Helpline 1930.
